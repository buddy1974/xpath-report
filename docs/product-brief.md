# X-PATH — Product Brief

**Platform:** X-PATH (xpath.report)
**Client / first tenant:** X.PATH Labs @ BettaHealth, Yaoundé, Cameroon.
**Holding:** BettaHealth (Betta Health Ltd), registered Dubai, operating Cameroon.
**Builder:** Maxpromo Digital (Marcel Tabit Akwe), Germany — §19 UStG (no VAT).

## Mission (client's words)
Move pathologists away from writing long essays — slow and prone to missing
details — toward a modern, structured, problem-solving way of producing the
Befund. Complete, structured, faster reports.

## What it is
A **multi-tenant professional platform for pathologists** (not a single-lab
tool). Each pathologist has a private, encrypted professional workspace where
they report (structured, voice-driven, reflex-checked), keep notes and learning
material, and share results with doctors of their choice. BettaHealth is tenant
one; the platform is built to serve pathologists and doctors across Cameroon and
beyond.

## Non-negotiables (see PROJECT_HEADER.md, authoritative)
- **Advisory, never diagnostic.** The pathologist interprets and signs; X-PATH
  structures, checks, codes, suggests. Never interprets patient images.
- **Private workspace vs clinical record.** Personal workspace is owner-only
  (not even the lab owner sees it). A signed report enters the audited clinical
  record of truth.
- **Templates: logic only**, never copied CAP/WHO/AJCC content.
- **Scope:** morphology + IHC in-house; molecular is send-out, separate, and
  surfaced only as advisory recommendation.
- **EN/FR + regional** intelligence (TB, schistosomiasis, Burkitt, etc.).

## Scope discipline
Build the engine + 3–5 real templates matching X.PATH's caseload and its 38
in-house antibodies. ~130 CAP templates and the 10-agent PRD are roadmap,
loaded as configuration over time.
